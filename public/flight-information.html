Participant Requirements

🪪 Identification

Identification Card or Passport

👕 Dress Code

Wear comfortable clothing, long trousers or jeans and covered shoes.
Please bring your NRIC or passport on the flight day.

📏 Height Requirement

Height : 152cm-193cm

⚖️ Weight Requirement

Weight : Max 3 passenger with combine weight not more than 230kg.

👦 Age Requirement

🔹Co-pilot participants must be at least 12 years old
🔹Passengers must be at least 5 years old.
🔹Pilot training should be at least 18 years old
Participants above 60 years old may be asked for a medical report.

📢 Meeting Point Confirmation

Subang Airport has multiple operating areas. Your final meeting point will be confirmed via WhatsApp before your flight day. Please do not proceed directly to Terminal Meeting Point or Office Meeting Point unless instructed by our team.

All flight experiences operate from Sultan Abdul Aziz Shah Airport (Subang Airport).

Please arrive at least 60 minutes before your scheduled flight.

Your final meeting point will be confirmed by our team before your flight.

✈ Important Information

Safety & Compliance

Flights are conducted by certified pilots in accordance with applicable aviation safety requirements. Aircraft are maintained and operated in compliance with relevant aviation regulations.

Insurance & Membership

All flights are covered by aviation insurance. Participants are required to complete the necessary Social Member registration with the flying club prior to the flight experience.

Future Pathways

Participants interested in aviation may continue their journey through flying clubs, recognised flight schools and pilot licensing pathways. Our team will be happy to share available options after your experience.

Operational Notice

Flight operations are subject to weather conditions, air traffic control requirements and overall safety considerations. Safety will always take priority over scheduling.
